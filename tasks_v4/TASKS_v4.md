## Text-channel inputs (cif, cif_raw)

For v4, BOTH text channels drop every `_geom_hbond_*` loop (`strip_hbond_loops` in `crystalsight/channels/cif_text.py`; all other lines byte-identical, `_geom_bond`/`_geom_angle` kept). Reason: these precomputed D-H...A tables carry CIF symmetry codes, which directly give the G1 acceptor copy and the edges of the G2/G3 H-bond graph. 11 of the 20 v4 tasks had such a table. `cif` additionally applies `strip_cif` (metadata removal). On 2026-10-22 `strip_cif` was fixed: the old version removed the `loop_` keyword of almost every loop (atom sites, symmetry operations, bonds) and dropped the line after each stripped `;` block. Text-channel results produced before that date (v3; v4 deepseek `cif`; the superseded v4 gpt-6-luna `cif`/`cif_raw`) used the old stripper and still contained the H-bond tables.
